# PixelPocket — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

PixelPocket ("the app") is a photo and video gallery for Android. This policy explains what
information the app handles and how.

## Information the app collects

**Registration details.** On first launch the app asks for your name, email address and,
optionally, your mobile number. These details are stored **only on your device** (in the app's
private storage) and are used only to personalise the app (for example, greeting you by name
and showing your profile in Settings). They are never uploaded, sold or shared.

**Photos and videos.** With your permission, the app reads the photos and videos on your device
so it can display, sort, crop, move, delete and share them. All of this happens **on your
device**. The app does not upload your media anywhere.

**Preferences.** Your save settings (image format, quality, album name) and grid layout are
stored on your device.

## Information the app does not collect

- The app has **no internet access** and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

## Sharing

When you choose **Share**, the selected photos or videos are handed to the app you pick
(for example, a messaging app) through Android's share sheet. What happens next is governed by
that app's privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or storage on older Android)** —
  to show your gallery.
- **Modify / delete media** — Android asks you to confirm each time you move or delete items.

## Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android
Settings, permanently deletes your registration details and preferences. Your photos and
videos are not affected by uninstalling.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
